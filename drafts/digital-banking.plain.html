Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

What is Digital Banking?
Digital Banking gives you secure access to your accounts through our website and mobile app. You can manage your finances, pay bills, transfer money, deposit checks, and more — anytime, anywhere.
How do I enroll in Digital Banking?
You can enroll by visiting our website and clicking 'Enroll' or by downloading our Mobile Banking app and following the setup instructions. You will need your account number and personal information to complete the enrollment.
What security measures are in place?
We use multiple layers of security including encryption, multi-factor authentication, automatic session timeouts, and real-time fraud monitoring to keep your accounts safe.
What is the cost for Digital Banking?
Digital Banking is free to use. There is no charge for enrolling in or using Online and Mobile Banking. Standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply.
What are the benefits of Digital Banking?
Digital Banking lets you bank on your schedule — 24/7 access to your accounts, instant alerts, paperless statements, easy bill pay, quick money transfers, mobile check deposit, and financial planning tools.
How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, go to the sign-in page and select 'Forgot Password'. You will be asked to verify your identity, then you can create a new password.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open an account?

You can open an account online in minutes using our quick and easy process. Just visit bankofamerica.com, click "Open an Account," and follow the prompts. We'll help guide you through each step.

Is my account secure?

Yes, we use industry-leading security measures including advanced encryption, fraud monitoring, and two-factor authentication to protect your account and personal information.

What mobile banking features are available?

Our mobile app offers deposit checks, transfer money, pay bills, check balances, locate ATMs, and more—all from your smartphone. Download the Bank of America Mobile App from your device's app store.

Can I transfer money internationally?

Yes, we offer international wire transfers and foreign exchange services. You can set up international transfers through online banking or by contacting your local branch for assistance.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I open an account?

    Opening an account is quick and easy. Visit our website, complete the online application, and you can start banking within minutes.

    What are the fees?

    We offer competitive pricing with no monthly maintenance fees on most accounts. Some ATM fees may apply for out-of-network transactions.

    Is my money safe?

    All deposits are insured by the FDIC up to $250,000. We also use industry-leading encryption and security protocols to protect your information.

    Can I access my account on mobile?

    Yes, our mobile app is available on iOS and Android with full banking features including transfers, bill pay, and check deposit.

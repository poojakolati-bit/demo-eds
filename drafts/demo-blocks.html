Frequently Asked Questions
How do I open an account?
You can open an account online in minutes using our quick and easy process. Just visit bankofamerica.com, click "Open an Account," and follow the prompts to get started.
Is my account secure?
Yes, we use industry-leading security measures including advanced encryption, fraud monitoring, and two-factor authentication to protect your account and personal information 24/7.
What mobile banking features are available?
Our mobile app offers deposit checks, transfer money, pay bills, check balances, locate ATMs, and more—all from your smartphone. Download the Bank of America Mobile App today.
Can I transfer money internationally?
Yes, we offer international wire transfers and foreign exchange services. You can set up international transfers through online banking or by contacting your local branch.
What are the account fees?
Bank of America offers accounts with flexible fee structures. Most checking accounts have no monthly maintenance fee when you meet minimum balance requirements or set up direct deposit.
